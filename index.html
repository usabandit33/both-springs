When one spring snaps, ask about both.

Chicago homeowners keep paying a second trip because the matching spring was left on the shaft. A national form will not write that checklist. This page does.

  • Confirm it is a torsion pair, not a single spring or extension springs.
  • Ask whether both springs are replaced on this visit.
  • Ask whether the trip / service-call fee credits if you approve the job.
  • Ask for the cycle rating they will install (and keep the old tags if they will leave them).
  • Ask who is coming: a local truck or a lead network that resells the call.
  • Get the arrival window and the after-hours rate in writing before anyone winds the bars.
